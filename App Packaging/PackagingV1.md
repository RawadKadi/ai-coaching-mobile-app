If your coaches conduct high-touch 1-on-1 coaching with **3 to 5 one-hour video sessions per client per week**, the video minute consumption is vastly higher than standard SaaS check-in models.

Here is the exact mathematical reality, why your previous model would burn cash, and how to structure client limits so you **never lose money**.

---

### 🧮 The True Math: High-Touch Coaching Video Consumption

* **1-on-1 Call**: 1 Coach + 1 Client = **2 participants**.
* **1 Session (1 Hour)**: 60 mins $\times$ 2 participants = **120 participant-minutes per session**.

#### Weekly & Monthly Consumption Per Client:

* **3 Sessions / Week**: 12 hours/month = **1,440 participant-minutes / month PER CLIENT**.
* **5 Sessions / Week**: 20 hours/month = **2,400 participant-minutes / month PER CLIENT**.

#### Cost to You (at Stream Video HD Rate of $0.0015 / participant-minute):

* **1 Client (3 hrs/wk)**: ~$2.16 / month in video fees.
* **1 Client (5 hrs/wk)**: ~$3.60 / month in video fees.
* **5 Clients (5 hrs/wk)**: **12,000 participant-minutes = ~$18.00 / month JUST IN VIDEO FEES.**

If you charge a coach **$25/month** for 5 clients doing 5 calls/week, after video fees ($18.00) + Gemini AI vision API fees ($4.00) + Supabase hosting ($1.00), your profit is **$2.00**. That is financially unsustainable.

---

## 🎯 The Fix: Tighten Client Roster Caps per Tier

To run a high-margin SaaS when coaches hold 3–5 live sessions a week, **client limits per package must be strictly capped**.

```
        FREE TIER                   STARTER                     PRO
        ($0 / mo)                  ($25 / mo)               ($59 / mo)
┌──────────────────────┐   ┌──────────────────────┐   ┌──────────────────────┐
│ • 1 Active Client    │   │ • Up to 2 Clients    │   │ • Up to 6 Clients    │
│ • 1 AI Meal Scan/day │   │ • Full AI Access     │   │ • Full AI Access     │
│ • External Video     │   │ • In-App Video       │   │ • In-App Video       │
│   (Zoom/Google Meet) │   │   (Max 2,500 Mins)   │   │   (Max 8,000 Mins)   │
└──────────────────────┘   └──────────────────────┘   └──────────────────────┘

```

---

### 1. Free Tier — "Starter Sandbox" ($0 / month)

> **Goal**: Let coaches onboard **1 client** to test the app, AI features, and flow without consuming your video budget.

* **Client Limit**: **1 Active Client** (Strict DB Cap).
* **Video Strategy**: **External Meeting Links Only** (Coach inputs their Zoom or Google Meet link into the app, tapping "Join Session" opens Zoom/Meet).
* *Why?* Allows $0 cost for you while coaches test the UI and AI meal/workout generators with their first client.


* **AI Capabilities**: Capped at **10 AI photo scans / month** + 3 AI Workout generations.
* **Your Infrastructure Cost**: **~$0.30 / month**.

---

### 2. Starter Tier — "Solo High-Touch Coach" ($25 / month)

> **Goal**: Protect margins for coaches who run high-frequency (3–5x/week) 1-on-1 video sessions.

* **Client Limit**: **2 Active Clients Maximum**.
* **Video Allocation**: **2,500 participant-minutes / month** (~20 hours of 1-on-1 clock time).
* *Covers*: 2 clients holding ~2.5 to 3 hours of live calls per week.


* **AI Capabilities**: 150 AI Meal Scans/mo + Unlimited Workout Generations.
* **Unit Economics**:
* **Your Video Cost**: ~$3.75
* **Your AI + DB Cost**: ~$2.50
* **Your Net Profit**: **~$18.75 / month (~75% Gross Margin)**.



---

### 3. Pro Tier — "Pro Studio" ($59 / month)

> **Goal**: Monetize coaches managing small active rosters of high-ticket, high-touch clients.

* **Client Limit**: **6 Active Clients Maximum**.
* **Video Allocation**: **8,000 participant-minutes / month** (~66 hours of 1-on-1 clock time).
* *Covers*: 6 clients holding ~2.5 to 3 hours of live calls per week.


* **AI Capabilities**: Unlimited AI Meal Scans & Analytics.
* **Unit Economics**:
* **Your Video Cost**: ~$12.00
* **Your AI + DB Cost**: ~$5.00
* **Your Net Profit**: **~$42.00 / month (~71% Gross Margin)**.



---

### 4. Scale / Unlimited Tier — "Agency / Enterprise" ($149 / month)

> **Goal**: For coaches with 7+ clients or high-volume sub-coaches.

* **Client Limit**: **Up to 15 Active Clients**.
* **Video Allocation**: **20,000 participant-minutes / month** (~166 hours of 1-on-1 clock time).
* **Your Infrastructure Cost**: ~$35.00.
* **Your Net Profit**: **~$114.00 / month**.

---

## 🛠️ Summary & Strategic Takeaway

By adjusting client capacity from **5 clients to 2 clients** on the $25 plan and **20 clients to 6 clients** on the $59 plan, your software aligns directly with the actual operational load of **3–5 one-hour calls per client per week** without putting your business in the red.
